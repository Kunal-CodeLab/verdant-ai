Nitrogen Starvation & Hypoxic Root Stress

Taxa: Solanaceae / Capsicum / Lycopersicon

Etiology & Symptomatology

Chlorotic discoloration across older leaves indicates nitrogen translocation caused by root-zone hypoxia resulting from oversaturated substrate.

Remediation Protocol
  • Suspend supplemental irrigation until the upper 50mm substrate reaches field capacity.
  • Perform manual radial root zone aeration 100mm from crown.
  • Incorporate aerated compost tea to restore mycorrhizal colonization.
